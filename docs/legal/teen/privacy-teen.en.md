This section is part of the Droblet Privacy Policy and is for you if you are 16 or 17. Everything in the main policy also
applies to you; here we explain, in plain words, only **what is different for you**. If anything is unclear, write to us:
[CONTACT E-MAIL].

**Data controller:** Droblet Ltd, [COMPANY ADDRESS], London, United Kingdom · Company no. [COMPANY NUMBER]

## IN SHORT

- At 16 or 17 you use Droblet in **teen mode**. If you are under 16 you cannot open an account.
- For the photo and AI features, **your parent or legal guardian** has to approve by e-mail. Until then you can build your
  wardrobe by hand or from the library and make looks and plans.
- Teen mode has **no social features**: nobody can find you, your profile and closet are invisible to others, and
  following, ratings, the feed and sharing are off.
- **No shopping**: no store or brand links, no cart, no purchases, no membership screen. Droblet is free for you.
- **No analytics, no ads, no marketing notifications.** We do not measure how you use the app.
- Your personal photos (body, face, try-on images, palette result, profile picture) are visible only to you, and are
  deleted automatically if you do not open the app for **90 days**.
- Your parent can withdraw their approval at any time; your personal photos are then deleted.
- When you turn 18 your account becomes a normal (adult) account and your parent's e-mail address is deleted.

## 1. Why and how we ask your age

When you sign up we ask your **birth year**, and your **birth month** only when the year alone is not enough (for example
if you turn 16 or 18 this year). We never ask your birthday. We keep only your **age band** ("teen" or "adult") and your
birth year (and month, if we asked), to apply the right rules and to work out the day you turn 18.

If you tell us you are under 16, no account is created and we do not keep what you entered. If you signed in with Apple or
Google and you are under 16, the account that sign-in created is deleted at once.

Your age is self-declared; we do not check ID. We expect you to tell the truth about your age.

## 2. Your parent's role

Uploading photos, the colour palette, the AI stylist, packing, item recognition and a profile picture need **your parent's
or legal guardian's approval**. You enter their e-mail address and we send them an approval link (valid for 7 days; you can
resend it). On the page your parent reads what Droblet is and what data is processed and, if they agree, ticks three
separate boxes.

- We keep your parent's e-mail address **only** to send these approval e-mails — never ads or newsletters.
- Your parent **cannot see your wardrobe, photos or activity**; Droblet is not a monitoring tool. Your parent can only
  approve or withdraw approval.
- In the approval records we keep only a one-way hash (SHA-256) of the e-mail address and of the IP address, which text was
  approved and when; we never store the IP address itself.
- If your parent declines or later withdraws, you can send a new request (for example to your other parent).

## 3. What data we process

The data in the main policy (account, profile, style and body profile, wardrobe, planning) applies to you too, with these
differences:

- **Age:** age band, birth year (and month if needed), the date you become an adult.
- **Parental approval:** your parent's e-mail address (only to send e-mails), approval status and history (event, time,
  hashes of the e-mail and IP address, text version, whether the try-on box was ticked).
- **What we do not collect:** social data (follows, ratings, saved looks, @handle), contact matching, shopping data (cart,
  watch list, favourites, clicks), purchases and membership, product analytics (Firebase) and our own usage events. The app
  does not send these for you, and the server would not store them anyway.

## 4. What is off for you, and why

| Off | Why |
|---|---|
| Follow, followers, people search, profile card, ratings, feed, share links, invite links, finding friends from contacts, @handle | So strangers cannot find or contact you. Your closet is always **private** and cannot be changed. You can still **block** anyone. |
| Store and brand links, cart, purchases, membership and credit purchases | So we never sell to you or send you to advertising. Teen mode is free. |
| Firebase product analytics and our own usage measurement | To collect no more data than needed. |
| Marketing notifications (weekly edit, price drops) | So we never market to you. Service notifications such as the morning outfit can stay on. |
| Trying on an outfit from a photo of another person | So photos of other people are not processed. |

## 5. AI and photos

- **Stylist:** gives you age- and school-appropriate suggestions; it leaves out revealing pieces such as swimwear and never
  uses suggestive wording. We give the AI extra instructions for this and check its answers.
- **Photos:** after your parent approves you can upload a body and a face photo. The rules of the main policy apply:
  location and device data are removed, the photo is stored encrypted and is **never shown to anyone else in any
  setting**. For AI processing your photo is sent to OpenAI (US); our data is stored with AWS in Frankfurt (Germany).
- **"See it on me" (virtual try-on):** currently **off** in teen mode. If it is switched on later it works only when all
  three hold: (1) we have switched the feature on, (2) your parent ticked the **separate** try-on box on the approval page,
  (3) you gave your own separate consent. Images are always fully clothed, in a neutral pose and keep your real age; your
  body is never commented on.

## 6. How long we keep data

- The periods in the main policy apply; in addition:
- **Personal photos** (body and face photo, try-on images, palette result): deleted automatically after **90 days** without
  activity. Signing in, or the app refreshing its session, counts as activity.
- **Your parent's e-mail address:** deleted when you turn 18 or delete your account.
- **Approval history** (hashes, time, text version): while the account exists; after 18 only as history.
  [LEGAL REVIEW: must a minimal consent record be kept after account deletion as evidence, and for how long?]
- **Requests not decided:** closed as "expired" after 7 days.

## 7. If your parent withdraws approval

Your parent can withdraw approval **at any time** through the link in their confirmation e-mail. Then:

- the photo and AI features switch off again;
- **your body and face photos, all try-on images, your palette result and your profile picture are deleted**;
- the garment images in your wardrobe, your looks and plans stay;
- we tell you in the app.

## 8. Your rights

The rights in the main policy (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, withdrawing consent, complaint) are
yours and you can use them **yourself**; your parent may also be able to act for you.
[LEGAL REVIEW: can a 16–17 year-old exercise KVKK art. 11 / UK GDPR rights alone, and how do we verify a request made by a
legal representative?]

- You can delete your personal photos at any time under Profile › Privacy.
- You can delete your account in the app; you do not need your parent's permission.
- You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye or the Information Commissioner's Office
  (ICO) in the UK.

## 9. When you turn 18

On the day you turn 18 your account becomes an **adult account**:

- your parent's links end and their e-mail address is deleted;
- the teen restrictions are lifted, but **nothing switches on by itself**: your closet stays private and marketing stays
  off until you choose otherwise;
- we tell you in the app.
