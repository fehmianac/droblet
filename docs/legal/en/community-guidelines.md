# Community Guidelines

> Last updated: 23 November 2026

On Droblet you can browse your friends' closets, rate their looks and take inspiration from them. These guidelines exist so that everyone feels safe and comfortable in the community. They form part of the Terms of Use.

**Data controller:** Ala Tech Solutions LTD, Unit 501 Leroy House, 434-436 Essex Road, London N1 3FY, United Kingdom · Company no. 17100391 · info@alatech.co

## IN SHORT

- Share only your own clothes and your own looks; never upload other people's photos.
- Nudity, sexual content, hate, harassment, violence and misleading accounts are not allowed.
- Rate the look, not the person. Ratings are anonymous; don't misuse that.
- You can report and block a person or their content from the ⋯ menu on their profile.
- We review reports; we remove content that breaks the rules and may suspend accounts.

## 1. What the community shares

On Droblet other users can see (depending on your closet visibility setting):

- your profile card: username, display name, style tags, follower counts;
- your wardrobe items: the background-removed item image and the item's name;
- your looks and their average rating.

Your full-body and face photos, the images generated on you and your colour palette are never shared. Droblet has no comments or private messages.

## 2. What you can share

- Photos of clothes, shoes, bags and accessories you own or wear.
- Looks you create and the names you give them.
- A username and display name that reflect you and do not impersonate anyone else.

When you photograph an item, make sure no other people, documents or personal information are visible in the background. The app removes the background of item images; others see only this cleaned image.

## 3. What you must not share

- Nudity, sexual content, or sexualised use of underwear or swimwear photos;
- photos of anyone under 18, or any content relating to the sexual abuse of children (we report such content to the authorities);
- a photo of another person taken or uploaded without their consent;
- content that demeans, hates or discriminates on the basis of race, ethnic origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability, body or appearance — including usernames and look names;
- threats, harassment, bullying or content glorifying violence;
- accounts that impersonate another person, influencer or brand;
- spam, advertising, referral links, or names containing personal contact details (phone, address, e-mail);
- counterfeit, smuggled or illegal products, weapons or drugs;
- images protected by someone else's copyright (for example uploading a brand's campaign photo as if it were your own item). To add shop products to your look, use the shop option in the app.

## 4. Rating

You can rate looks from 1 to 5 stars. Ratings are anonymous: the look's owner sees only the average and how many people rated it, never who gave what.

A rating is an opinion on a look's style, not a judgement of a person's body or appearance. Mass or coordinated low ratings to target someone, rating from fake accounts and trading ratings are not allowed.

## 5. Respect and privacy

If an account is Private or Followers only, you cannot see the closet until your follow request is approved; don't keep repeating a declined request.

Do not use images from other people's closets or looks to humiliate or expose them, or for commercial purposes without permission.

## 6. Reporting

If you see a profile, item or look that breaks the rules, tap ⋯ › Report on the person's profile. Choose a reason: spam, inappropriate content, harassment, impersonation, minor safety or other. You can add a note of up to 500 characters.

The person you report is never told who you are. To prevent abuse, an account can send at most 20 reports a day; knowingly filing false reports also breaks these guidelines.

Our moderation team reviews reports. Reports about the safety of minors are prioritised.

If you cannot access the app or want to report something from outside it, write to info@alatech.co.

## 7. Blocking

You can block someone from their profile with ⋯ › Block. When you block someone:

- you cannot see each other in search, on profiles, in the feed, in suggestions or in contact matching;
- follow relationships and pending follow requests between you are removed in both directions;
- you cannot rate, report or follow each other.

The blocked person is not notified. You can see the people you blocked and unblock them under Profile › Settings; unblocking does not restore follow relationships.

## 8. Moderation and enforcement

A moderator reviews every report and decides on one of these outcomes:

- close the report (if no rule was broken);
- remove the content;
- warn the account owner;
- suspend the account — a suspended account cannot sign in.

For serious violations (for example child abuse or serious threats) we may close the account without warning and, where required, report it to the competent authorities. Moderators cannot see users' full-body or face photos or generated images; reviews are based only on content visible to others.

Repeated violations lead to stronger measures.

## 9. Appeals

If you think your content was removed or your account suspended by mistake, write to info@alatech.co with your username within 30 days of learning of the decision. Your appeal is reviewed by someone who did not make the first decision, and we tell you the outcome. [LEGAL REVIEW: appeal period]

## 10. Emergencies

If you think someone's life or safety is in danger, call the emergency services first: 112 in Türkiye, 999 in the United Kingdom. Droblet is not an emergency service.

## 11. Changes

We may update these guidelines as the community grows. We announce material changes in the app.
