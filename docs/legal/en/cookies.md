## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files saved in your browser when you visit a website. Similar technologies include the browser's local storage (localStorage, sessionStorage), apps storing data on the device and tracking pixels embedded in pages. In this notice “cookies” covers all of them.

## 2. What our website uses

droblet.alatech.co is a static website. The site's own code:

- does not set or read cookies;
- does not save data in localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB;
- contains no analytics, advertising, retargeting, social media plug-ins or tracking pixels;
- serves fonts and images from its own domain; opening a page sends no request to Google Fonts or any other third party.

The “Delete my account” and “Reset password” pages send what you enter to the Droblet API only to carry out that action. During account deletion the session token is kept only in the page's memory; it is never stored in a cookie or browser storage and is gone when you close the page.

On the link pages (invite, look, password reset) your browser's language setting is read only to send you to the right language page; it is not saved anywhere.

## 4. Mobile app

For product analytics it uses the Google Analytics for Firebase SDK, which keeps an app instance id and the events waiting to be sent on your device. You can switch it off under Settings › Privacy › “Product analytics” (Privacy Policy section 9). The usage events the app sends to our own servers are described in the Privacy Policy.
