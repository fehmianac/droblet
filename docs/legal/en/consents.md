## 2. Consent 1 — Full-body photo and “See it on me”

Purpose: to generate images that show outfits and shop products on you (“See it on me”).

Data: the full-body photo you upload and the images generated from it. The photo's location and device information is removed in the first processing step after upload.

Who processes it and where: Droblet Ltd; the photo is stored on AWS servers in Frankfurt (Germany) and sent to OpenAI (US) to generate the images.

How long: until you delete the photo or the generated images, or your account. The photo and the images are never shown to other users in any setting.

If you do not consent: you cannot use “See it on me”; wardrobe, looks, the stylist and the other features keep working.

Consent statement: “I give my explicit consent to Droblet Ltd processing my full-body photo to generate ‘See it on me’ images, and transferring it for that purpose to AWS (Germany) and OpenAI (US).”

## 3. Consent 2 — Face photo and colour palette analysis

Purpose: to find the colour season and shades that suit you by looking at the skin, hair and eye colours in your face photo (colour palette analysis).

Data: the face photo you upload and the palette result derived from it (season type, shades that suit you and shades to avoid). No identification, verification or face recognition takes place; no face template that uniquely identifies you is extracted or stored.

Who processes it and where: Droblet Ltd; the photo is stored on AWS servers in Frankfurt (Germany) and sent to OpenAI (US) for the analysis.

How long: until you delete the photo or your account. When you delete your face photo, the palette result is deleted too. The photo and the palette are never shown to other users in any setting.

If you do not consent: you cannot use the colour palette analysis; the other features keep working.

Consent statement: “I give my explicit consent to Droblet Ltd processing my face photo only for colour palette analysis and without identifying me, and transferring it for that purpose to AWS (Germany) and OpenAI (US).”

## 4. Consent 3 — Weight

How long: until you remove it or delete your account.

If you do not consent: you leave the weight field empty; no feature is affected.

Consent statement: “I give my explicit consent to Droblet Ltd storing my weight as part of my body profile.”

## 5. Consent 4 — Finding friends from your contacts

Purpose: to find people in your contacts who use Droblet.

Data: on your phone, the e-mail addresses in your contacts are turned into SHA-256 hashes with a single-use salt that changes on every request; only these hashes are sent. Names, phone numbers and the e-mail addresses themselves never leave your phone.

Who processes it and where: Droblet Ltd; the hashes are held in memory on AWS servers in Frankfurt (Germany) only while the match runs.

How long: the hashes are neither saved nor logged; the salt is deleted after use and expires within 10 minutes at the latest.

If you do not consent: your phone never asks for contacts permission; you can find friends by searching for their username or with an invite link.

Consent statement: “I give my explicit consent to the e-mail addresses in my contacts being turned into hashes on my phone and sent to Droblet Ltd to find my friends on Droblet.”

## 8. Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw any consent at any time:

- Full-body and face photo: delete the photo under Profile › Privacy; the related consent counts as withdrawn and the generated images or the palette result are deleted.
- Weight: clear the weight field in your body profile in the app.
- Contacts: switch off Droblet's contacts permission in your phone's settings.
- Marketing notifications: switch off the notification under Profile › Settings › Notifications.
- In any other case, or in addition to the above: write to [CONTACT E-MAIL].

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. [LEGAL REVIEW: consent withdrawal screen in the app]
