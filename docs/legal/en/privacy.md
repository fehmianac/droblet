## 4. Photos, your face photo and your weight

Uploading a full-body or face photo is optional; you can use the app without them. If you upload one, its location and device information (EXIF/XMP metadata) is removed in the first processing step, the clean copy is stored encrypted in storage that is closed to the public, and it is shown only to you, through private links that expire quickly.

We use your face photo for two purposes, each with its own explicit consent: the colour palette analysis (the AI looks at the skin, hair and eye colours in the photo and chooses the season type that suits you) and, when you have no full-body photo, “See it on me” (the AI combines your face photo with the body type, height and size you choose to build your figure). We do not identify or verify you or run face recognition; we do not extract or store a template that uniquely identifies you from your face (a biometric template). The AI is explicitly told not to identify, describe or change the person. For these reasons we consider that this is not processing of biometric data; even so, we process your face and full-body photos only with your explicit consent. ## 7. Finding friends from your contacts

This feature is optional. The app first explains what will happen and asks for your agreement; only if you agree does your phone ask for contacts permission.

On your phone, the e-mail addresses in your contacts are turned into SHA-256 hashes with a single-use salt that changes on every request. Only these hashes are sent to us; names, phone numbers and the e-mail addresses themselves never leave your phone.

We compare the hashes with values made the same way from our members' verified e-mail addresses and show you the profile cards of the members found (as far as their visibility settings allow). Uploaded hashes are neither saved nor logged; the salt is deleted after use and expires within 10 minutes at the latest. At most 10 matches can be run per day.

Phone numbers are not used by this feature. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings; as no contact data is stored, there is nothing to delete. If you do not want to be found from other people's contacts, the only way at the moment is to delete your account; a separate “don't match me” option may be added later.
