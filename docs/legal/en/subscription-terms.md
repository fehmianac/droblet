## 7. AI image allowance

Membership includes 30 AI images in every subscription month. One “See it on me” image uses 1 credit. The allowance is topped up to 30 at the start of each subscription month; unused images do not roll over. On the yearly plan the allowance also renews monthly.

Before a task that will create several images (for example every day of the weekly plan), the app shows how many credits it will use and asks you to confirm. If the image of the same outfit is requested again, it comes from the cache and no second credit is used. If an image cannot be created, the credit is returned.

You can see your remaining credits on the Profile screen.
