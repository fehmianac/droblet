## English

**Subtitle (≤ 30):** `AI wardrobe & outfit planner` (28)

**Promotional text (≤ 170):**
`Photograph your clothes; Droblet prepares your outfit every morning for the weather and your day — and packs your suitcase for every trip.` (138)

**Keywords (≤ 100, comma-separated, no spaces):**
`closet,style,stylist,fashion,clothes,packing,suitcase,lookbook,colour palette,capsule,ootd,travel` (97)

**Description:**

```
No more "what do I wear" mornings or hours of packing. Your wardrobe now thinks for you.

Photograph your clothes or pick them from the ready-made library; Droblet recognises the type, colour and fabric and organises your wardrobe by category and season. Then it prepares your outfit for every day and every trip.

OUTFIT OF THE DAY
Every morning, a ready outfit from your own wardrobe, matched to the weather and the occasion in your calendar. Not feeling it? One tap for another idea — and plan the whole week together.

STYLIST
Say "office tomorrow" or "wedding on Saturday" and get three different outfits from the clothes you own. If a piece is missing, we suggest one in your size and budget.

SEE IT ON ME
See outfits and store products on your own photo. AI-generated images are always labelled "On you" and are visible only to you.

SMART SUITCASE
Enter the city and dates: you get daily looks for the forecast and a packing list where every piece is worn more than once. There is a single-cabin-bag mode too.

COLOUR PALETTE
Finds your season type and the tones that suit you best from a face photo, and shows how well your wardrobe matches your palette.

STYLE SHOP
The weekly edit, brand products and a watchlist that tells you when a price drops. Match a product with your wardrobe before you buy; you check out in the brand's own store.

FRIENDS
Follow friends and rate their outfits. You choose who can see your wardrobe; you can block anyone and report inappropriate content.

PRIVACY
Your photos are processed only for your account. Body and face photos each need their own explicit consent; you can withdraw consent, delete your photos and close your account in the app at any time.

DROBLET MEMBERSHIP
Building your wardrobe, saving looks and following friends are free. Membership adds 30 AI images a month (See it on me), the personal stylist and weekly plan, smart packing, colour palette analysis and unlimited piece recognition. Monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial for new members. Extra AI image packs are available separately.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription.

Terms of Use: https://droblet.alatech.co/en/terms
Subscription Terms: https://droblet.alatech.co/en/subscription-terms
Privacy Policy: https://droblet.alatech.co/en/privacy

For ages 18 and over. Women's wardrobe for now; men's and teens' coming soon.
```
